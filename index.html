Selected Projects

GoT-Diffuse: Compositional Text-to-Image Generation

SDXL · Diffusion · PyTorch

Identified a planner–decoder execution gap in reasoning-based text-to-image on T2I-CompBench++ (plans spatially valid 83.1% of the time, but only 64.7% of images satisfy the relations) via a custom BLIP-VQA/UniDet diagnostic. Layout-guided SDXL conditioning lifts spatial relation preservation +19% and color binding +15%, motivating a timestep-routed diffusion decoder that preserves object-specific conditioning during early denoising.

TS-QFormer: Time-Series-to-LLM Adapter for QA

PyTorch · Frozen Encoders/LLMs

A parameter-efficient adapter connecting a frozen MOMENT-large time-series encoder to a frozen OPT-2.7B language model via channel-aware projections and question-conditioned query tokens, with a supervised chain-of-thought pipeline for temporal reasoning that improves ROUGE-F1 over a direct-answer baseline.
